Our Mission: Developing digital talent to match the skills employers need
Our Story
AD2000 Digital Productions is a newly formed Social Enterprise, providing employment opportunities and training for talented young people who want to pursue a career in the digital production and social media marketing industry. AD2000 Digital Productions' founder has over 30 years' experience assisting young people from disadvantaged social and economic backgrounds through AD 2000 Productions, a community-led media project based in Edmonton Green. We saw that the advent of the internet during the 1990s would change the way the world works, learns, socialises and researches information. So we offered multimedia, internet, ICT and video production training to young people from disadvantaged backgrounds to help them benefit from these new opportunities. Many of our students went on to work in the television and multimedia industries, either as staff or freelancers. Although we were very successful in assisting our students to gain their first recognised vocational qualification and get into employment, AD 2000 Productions had to disband in 2001, due to changes in government and local policies on funding community-led projects. We now see an opportunity to establish a Social Enterprise to advance the work that we previously did to assist disadvantaged young people to gain digital media skills that will enable them to compete for jobs. As the country moves out of recession, there is an ongoing need for talented people to fill the vacancies that will grow this dynamic, evolving industry. AD2000 Digital Productions will exploit the internet to reach individuals who want to learn and develop digital skills and pursue a career in the digital media industry. We have also developed an e-learning platform for our students to receive personalised learning to help them advance in their chosen careers. As a Social Enterprise, AD2000 Digital Productions will not depend on grants to maintain its long-term sustainability and to fulfil its vision of delivering quality digital media training which is cost effective and produces results. Alongside and supporting our training activity, we also have a digital production facility, specialising in producing web videos, interactive learning resources and websites. Our production team has many years' experience producing quality personalised videos, specialising in social media and e-learning technologies, post-production techniques and project management.
