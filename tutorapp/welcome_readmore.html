Welcome to CareerPaths Online

Careerpathsonline supports students, parents and schools by providing flexible education and career development programmes for the future.

We aim to improve education opportunities for all individuals. Some students feel that they missed the chance to make the most of their education. We know that it is never too late to grasp opportunities but it can seem daunting. A little help can go a long way to add the confidence to succeed.

Some youngsters find it hard to settle within a school environment. We can help them to access materials so that they can make progress and achieve a brighter future with a better understanding of the world around them. In this way they will be better prepared for the challenges of a working life.

CareerPaths Online team consists of trainers, educational psychologist, multimedia developers, course designers and programmers. They understand how people learn. Our content is structured and paced so that users are able to understand, absorb and retain the learning points and rapidly put them into practice.
